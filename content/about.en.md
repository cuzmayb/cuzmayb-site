# Hi, I'm Hyojun Jung.

I am a senior in Materials Science and Chemical Engineering at Hanyang University ERICA. I graduate in February 2027, with a GPA of <span class="nw">4.05/4.5</span>.

I want to solve the problems that come up in semiconductor advanced packaging from the materials and process side: thin films, etch and interconnects. Right now I am building projects that tackle process problems with Python and AI tools.

What I am most curious about is how a single process condition changes quality at the package and system level. As I fill in the skills the job needs, I record here where I got stuck, why I decided what I did, and what I learned.

## The name cuzmayb

It comes from "because, maybe" in Oasis's "Wonderwall". Cuz is short for because.

Like Bellingham at the 2026 World Cup, I want to be the one who finds the answer when things are stuck.

## What I am thinking about lately

### Is a process margin set on a few coupons still right at volume?

In the lab, the limits of a process condition are set on a handful of coupons. Whether that headroom also covers the spread you get at volume has to be checked separately. I am working out which failure modes to look at first, so that a margin from few experiments can be trusted.

### How do I check a simulation result on real parts?

A simulation answers within the values it assumes. If the spread in real products is larger than assumed, a detection limit or process window from the calculation does not hold as it is. I want to know what to measure on real parts, and how much, before a model can be trusted.

### What do I like, and what do I not know?

Before deciding what work to do, I want to see clearly what I like, and what I know and do not know. That is also why I write down where I got stuck on every project.
